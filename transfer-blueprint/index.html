Where Value Meets Excellence

Why we are
not $30,000.

We recognize that admissions consulting is a significant investment. Families should not have to choose between exceptional mentorship and a pricing model that respects the value of their resources.

Full Axis was built around a simple principle: students should invest in strategy, mentorship, and personalized guidance, not excessive overhead, inflated branding, or costs that can rival portions of a college education.

In an industry where consulting packages can often reach tens of thousands of dollars, a lower price can create the assumption that quality must be sacrificed. We believe the opposite. Our pricing is not a discounted version of traditional consulting. It is a different, more intentional business model designed around maximizing the impact students receive from their investment.

Part of what you're paying for elsewhere is tenure, a "10-year regional advisor" whose expertise is measured in years since they last sat in an admissions seat. We think that's the wrong thing to charge a premium for. The real question isn't how many years someone has in the industry. It's how many years it's been since they were actually inside the room. Admissions criteria shift every cycle, and what got someone in five years ago isn't what's getting someone in now. Test-optional policies, the end of affirmative action, the rise of AI-written essays, and record application volumes have changed the admissions landscape entirely in just the past few years. A decade of "experience" can just as easily mean a decade of distance from how decisions are actually made right now. Every mentor at Full Axis was admitted to their target school recently enough to know exactly what worked this cycle. You're not paying for decades of distance from the process. You're paying for proximity to it, and that proximity isn't something we're planning to phase out as we grow.

As Full Axis grows, our network grows with it. New mentors join as they complete their own admissions journeys, each bringing firsthand knowledge of exactly what worked that cycle. The result is a network that never ages out of relevance. As the admissions landscape shifts, so does the Axis Network, always shaped by whoever just proved what it takes to get in.

That proximity also comes with commitment. Every mentor is contracted for the full cycle they're paired with you. No hand-offs, no new advisor learning your file from scratch halfway through. In the rare case something unexpected happens, another qualified mentor steps in without missing a beat. The same person who understands your goals in September is the one reviewing your final essays in January.

This isn't a smaller version of the traditional model. It's a bet that the entire industry got it backwards, that trust should be earned through proximity and results, not manufactured through decades of distance and a price tag. As Full Axis grows, that's the standard we intend to hold, and the standard we hope the rest of this industry eventually has to answer to.

Full Axis Fellowship

First-generation and low-income students deserve the same caliber of mentorship as any applicant. Every verified FGLI applicant is guaranteed a place in our program — as a Fellow, or on our Access Rate.

Who This Is For

Built for students
the system underestimates.

The Full Axis Fellowship exists for first-generation and low-income (FGLI) students who bring the same drive and potential as any applicant we work with, but not always the same resources. Every applicant who verifies as FGLI is guaranteed a place in our program — either as a Fellow or through our Access Rate.

How It Works

Every FGLI-verified applicant is considered for the Fellowship first. Students not selected as Fellows are automatically placed on our Access Rate — a reduced-cost tier across our full mentorship program. No verified applicant is turned away.

Our Philosophy

Financial background shouldn't determine access to strategy and mentorship. We built the Fellowship so our most resource-constrained applicants get the same caliber of guidance as any student at Full Axis.

FGLI Access Rate

Verified FGLI applicants who are not selected as Fellows are automatically placed on our Access Rate: two-thirds of standard pricing, applied to whichever Transfer Axis tier fits your needs.

The Rule
2/3
  • Access Rate = two-thirds of the standard price for any Transfer Axis tier
  • Applies to the exact package and add-ons you choose — nothing is pre-set
For Example
~33% Off
  • Founders ($1,500 standard) → ~$1,000 Access Rate
  • Premium ($3,000 standard) → ~$2,000 Access Rate

Review Axis — Access Rate

Essay-only pricing scales with your actual supplemental load, then the same two-thirds Access Rate applies to the total.

How the total is built: Your base package (High Schoolers $125 / Transfers $75) covers your first one to two essays. Every additional standard supplemental adds $50, and every essay under 100 words adds $25. A heavy supplemental load — for example, a school list weighted toward HYPSM, which can run around 25 total supplements — typically totals somewhere between $1,000 and $2,000 at standard rates, depending on the exact mix of full-length and short-response essays.

Access Rate applicants pay two-thirds of that final total — so a $1,500 standard total becomes roughly $1,000, and a $2,000 standard total becomes roughly $1,330. We calculate your exact total during your free consultation, once we know your actual school list.

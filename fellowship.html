Who This Is For

Built for students
the system underestimates.

The Full Axis Fellowship exists for first-generation and low-income (FGLI) students who bring the same drive and potential as any applicant we work with, but not always the same resources. Every applicant who verifies as FGLI is guaranteed a place in our program, either as a Fellow or through our Access Rate.

How It Works

Every FGLI-verified applicant is considered for the Fellowship first. Students not selected as Fellows are automatically placed on our Access Rate, a reduced-cost tier across our full mentorship program. No verified applicant is turned away.

Our Philosophy

Financial background shouldn't determine access to strategy and mentorship. We built the Fellowship so our most resource-constrained applicants get the same caliber of guidance as any student at Full Axis.

Fellowship Tier

Full Axis Fellowship

A limited number of Fellowship spots are available each cycle. Selected Fellows pay a single flat intro fee and are matched into our Founders package, with standout applicants occasionally placed into Premium at no added cost. Selection is based on need and fit, not first-come-first-served.

Fellows are selected through a short need-and-fit review during the free consultation: we look at financial background, first-gen status, and where a dedicated mentor would make the biggest difference. There's no separate essay or additional fee to apply; it's part of the same intake process every applicant goes through.

Not selected as a Fellow this cycle? You're automatically placed on the Access Rate below — every verified FGLI applicant gets a place in the program, one way or another.

FGLI Access Rate

Verified FGLI applicants who are not selected as Fellows are automatically placed on our Access Rate, applied to whichever Transfer Axis tier fits your needs.

The rule is simple: you pay two thirds of the standard price for whichever tier and add ons you choose. Here's how that plays out across our packages:

  • Starter ($1,350 standard) becomes $900
  • Founders ($1,500 standard) becomes $1,000
  • Advanced ($2,000 standard) becomes $1,330
  • Premium ($3,000 standard) becomes $2,000
Nothing is pre set. Your Access Rate is calculated on the exact package and add ons you choose.

Review Axis — Access Rate

Essay-only pricing scales with your actual supplemental load, then the same two-thirds Access Rate applies to the total.

Here's how a heavy list, like a HYPSM-weighted school list with around 25 total supplements, typically builds up:

  • Base package (Transfers, $75): covers your first essay
  • 15 additional standard supplementals × $50 = $750
  • 9 short-response essays under 100 words × $25 = $225
  • Standard total: $75 + $750 + $225 = $1,050

Your actual mix of full-length and short-response essays will differ, so this is illustrative rather than fixed. We calculate your exact total during your free consultation, once we know your real school list.

Access Rate applicants pay two-thirds of the final total. In this example, the $1,050 standard total becomes roughly $700.

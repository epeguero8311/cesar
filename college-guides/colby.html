Overview

Colby College's first-year admissions process is evaluated separately from its transfer process, with its own applicant pool, review committee, and timeline. Most highly selective schools admit first-year applicants at a meaningfully different rate than transfers, so the numbers above should not be assumed to mirror the transfer figures below.

Applicants generally apply through Early Decision, Early Action, or Regular Decision depending on the school's specific offerings, and should weigh the tradeoffs of each round carefully, since binding Early Decision plans and non-binding Early Action or Restrictive Early Action plans have different implications for financial aid and other applications.

What Colby Looks For

  • A rigorous high school course load, including the most advanced classes available (AP, IB, honors, or dual enrollment)
  • Strong, consistent grades throughout high school, particularly in junior and senior year
  • Meaningful extracurricular involvement, leadership, or personal responsibilities that show depth rather than a long list of shallow activities
  • Thoughtful, authentic essays that reveal genuine voice and self-reflection
  • Strong letters of recommendation from teachers and counselors who know the applicant well

Requirements & Deadlines

  • Common Application, Coalition Application, or the school's own first-year application, submitted by the relevant deadline above
  • Official high school transcript and school profile
  • Letters of recommendation from a counselor and one or more teachers
  • Standardized testing policy varies by school and by year — confirm current SAT/ACT requirements before applying
  • Decisions released according to the applicant's chosen round (Early Decision/Action vs. Regular Decision)

Overview

Colby generally admits somewhere around 10-15% of transfer applicants each year, filling only about 10-15 spots out of applicant pools in the 100-320 range — a highly selective process even by Colby's own competitive standards.

Colby accepts transfer applicants for both fall and spring terms, with fall carrying the larger intake. The college is test-optional and gives priority in admissions to incoming first-year students, with transfer spots filled as space allows.

What Colby Looks For

  • Strong academic and personal records from an accredited college or university
  • An academic evaluation from a full-time professor who has recently instructed the applicant
  • A Transfer College Report completed by the registrar or a similar campus administrator
  • TOEFL, IELTS, or Duolingo scores if English is not the applicant's first or current language of instruction
  • A specific, well-articulated reason for choosing Colby's academic environment

Requirements & Deadlines

  • Common Application, due March 1 for fall entry or November 1 for spring entry
  • Official transcripts from every college attended, plus a current college transcript
  • Academic evaluation and Transfer College Report
  • Test-optional; self-reported SAT/ACT scores accepted
  • Decisions released on a rolling basis

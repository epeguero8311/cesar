Overview

Bowdoin College's first-year admissions process is evaluated separately from its transfer process, with its own applicant pool, review committee, and timeline. Most highly selective schools admit first-year applicants at a meaningfully different rate than transfers, so the numbers above should not be assumed to mirror the transfer figures below.

Applicants generally apply through Early Decision, Early Action, or Regular Decision depending on the school's specific offerings, and should weigh the tradeoffs of each round carefully, since binding Early Decision plans and non-binding Early Action or Restrictive Early Action plans have different implications for financial aid and other applications.

What Bowdoin Looks For

  • A rigorous high school course load, including the most advanced classes available (AP, IB, honors, or dual enrollment)
  • Strong, consistent grades throughout high school, particularly in junior and senior year
  • Meaningful extracurricular involvement, leadership, or personal responsibilities that show depth rather than a long list of shallow activities
  • Thoughtful, authentic essays that reveal genuine voice and self-reflection
  • Strong letters of recommendation from teachers and counselors who know the applicant well

Requirements & Deadlines

  • Common Application, Coalition Application, or the school's own first-year application, submitted by the relevant deadline above
  • Official high school transcript and school profile
  • Letters of recommendation from a counselor and one or more teachers
  • Standardized testing policy varies by school and by year — confirm current SAT/ACT requirements before applying
  • Decisions released according to the applicant's chosen round (Early Decision/Action vs. Regular Decision)

Overview

Bowdoin's transfer acceptance rate swings dramatically year to year based on available space — recent cycles have ranged from as low as 1-3% up to nearly 12%, with the college typically receiving 150-260 applications and admitting only 5-25 students.

Bowdoin does not offer a spring entry term for transfers. Any student who has been enrolled in a degree-granting program elsewhere must apply as a transfer, and admission is considered extremely competitive given the tiny number of available seats.

What Bowdoin Looks For

  • Honors-quality college work, generally B-level (3.0) or better, though there is no formal GPA cutoff
  • Both high school and college transcripts, since Bowdoin reviews academic history holistically
  • Two academic evaluations from instructors who have taught the applicant in college-level courses
  • A compelling personal statement explaining the reason for transferring specifically to Bowdoin
  • A record of engagement outside the classroom, since space is so limited that fit matters enormously

Requirements & Deadlines

  • Common Application for Transfer, due March 3 for fall entry
  • Official transcripts from every college attended, plus a final high school transcript
  • Two academic evaluations and a College Report from the current institution
  • Test-optional; self-reported or official SAT/ACT scores accepted
  • Decisions released on a rolling basis; no spring transfer term offered

Overview

UC Berkeley admits roughly a quarter of its transfer applicant pool most years, which is meaningfully higher than its single-digit first-year acceptance rate. Recent cycles have hovered in the 24–27% range, with Berkeley extending offers to somewhere between 4,000 and 5,500 transfer students annually out of applicant pools in the 19,000–21,500 range. The overwhelming majority of admits — over 90% in recent years — come from California community colleges, reflecting the UC system's priority on the community college transfer pathway.

Berkeley only accepts transfer applicants for fall entry, and does not use letters of recommendation or official transcripts during initial review — the personal insight questions and reported coursework carry the weight.

What Berkeley Looks For

  • Completion of all lower-division prerequisite coursework for the intended major before enrolling — major preparation matters more here than at most schools
  • A strong GPA in UC-transferable coursework, with competitive applicants often well above the stated 3.0 minimum
  • Completion of the required English composition and quantitative reasoning coursework
  • Compelling responses to the required major-readiness essay plus a set of personal insight questions

Requirements & Deadlines

  • University of California application, opening August 1 and due by November 30 for fall entry
  • Minimum 60 UC-transferable semester units (90 quarter units) completed by the end of spring term prior to enrollment
  • Transfer Academic Update (TAU) submitted by January 31, reporting fall grades and planned spring coursework
  • Decisions typically arrive in mid-to-late April, released directly through the applicant's UC portal

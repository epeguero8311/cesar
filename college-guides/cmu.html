Decision dates, requirements, and what the admissions committee actually prioritizes — for both first-year and transfer applicants.

Overview

Carnegie Mellon University's first-year admissions process is evaluated separately from its transfer process, with its own applicant pool, review committee, and timeline. Most highly selective schools admit first-year applicants at a meaningfully different rate than transfers, so the numbers above should not be assumed to mirror the transfer figures below.

Applicants generally apply through Early Decision, Early Action, or Regular Decision depending on the school's specific offerings, and should weigh the tradeoffs of each round carefully, since binding Early Decision plans and non-binding Early Action or Restrictive Early Action plans have different implications for financial aid and other applications.

What Carnegie Mellon Looks For

  • A rigorous high school course load, including the most advanced classes available (AP, IB, honors, or dual enrollment)
  • Strong, consistent grades throughout high school, particularly in junior and senior year
  • Meaningful extracurricular involvement, leadership, or personal responsibilities that show depth rather than a long list of shallow activities
  • Thoughtful, authentic essays that reveal genuine voice and self-reflection
  • Strong letters of recommendation from teachers and counselors who know the applicant well

Requirements & Deadlines

  • Common Application, Coalition Application, or the school's own first-year application, submitted by the relevant deadline above
  • Official high school transcript and school profile
  • Letters of recommendation from a counselor and one or more teachers
  • Standardized testing policy varies by school and by year — confirm current SAT/ACT requirements before applying
  • Decisions released according to the applicant's chosen round (Early Decision/Action vs. Regular Decision)

Overview

Carnegie Mellon admits a small number of transfer students each year strictly as space allows, rather than targeting a fixed acceptance rate. Recent cycles have generally landed between 7% and 11%, though the rate has occasionally spiked higher when more space opened up.

CMU offers both fall and spring transfer options, though spring transfer is far more limited due to space constraints, and College of Fine Arts applicants must instead meet first-year deadlines. Transfer applicants are considered for only one college or program within CMU and cannot apply to interdisciplinary BXA programs.

What Carnegie Mellon Looks For

  • Excellent, near-straight-A grades in college coursework — the average GPA among enrolling transfers has been reported around 3.75
  • A strong high school record and standardized test scores, since CMU still weighs secondary school performance alongside college work
  • A record of exceptional achievement outside the classroom, such as research, technology projects, or publications
  • Coursework and preparation specific to the single college or program the applicant is applying to, since transfer credit and admission are evaluated program by program
  • Thoughtful responses to CMU's required transfer essays explaining the applicant's academic passion and goals

Requirements & Deadlines

  • CMU's transfer application, due February 15 for fall entry or October 15 for spring entry (space-permitting)
  • Official transcripts from every college attended, plus course descriptions for all courses taken
  • Official SAT or ACT scores, if previously taken
  • One recommendation from a professor or advisor in place of a high school counselor recommendation
  • Decisions released in May for fall applicants

Overview

Boston University's first-year admissions process is evaluated separately from its transfer process, with its own applicant pool, review committee, and timeline. Most highly selective schools admit first-year applicants at a meaningfully different rate than transfers, so the numbers above should not be assumed to mirror the transfer figures below.

Applicants generally apply through Early Decision, Early Action, or Regular Decision depending on the school's specific offerings, and should weigh the tradeoffs of each round carefully, since binding Early Decision plans and non-binding Early Action or Restrictive Early Action plans have different implications for financial aid and other applications.

What BU Looks For

  • A rigorous high school course load, including the most advanced classes available (AP, IB, honors, or dual enrollment)
  • Strong, consistent grades throughout high school, particularly in junior and senior year
  • Meaningful extracurricular involvement, leadership, or personal responsibilities that show depth rather than a long list of shallow activities
  • Thoughtful, authentic essays that reveal genuine voice and self-reflection
  • Strong letters of recommendation from teachers and counselors who know the applicant well

Requirements & Deadlines

  • Common Application, Coalition Application, or the school's own first-year application, submitted by the relevant deadline above
  • Official high school transcript and school profile
  • Letters of recommendation from a counselor and one or more teachers
  • Standardized testing policy varies by school and by year — confirm current SAT/ACT requirements before applying
  • Decisions released according to the applicant's chosen round (Early Decision/Action vs. Regular Decision)

Overview

Boston University is one of the more transfer-friendly major research universities in the country. Recent cycles have put the transfer acceptance rate roughly in the high-20s to mid-30s — well above BU's freshman acceptance rate, which has fallen into the single digits for Regular Decision. BU typically admits somewhere in the 2,000–2,300 range out of applicant pools of 4,500–6,700.

Unlike most highly selective schools, BU accepts transfers for both fall and spring entry, though not every major or college is open to spring admission. Fall carries the larger intake and more program options. Decisions are released on a rolling basis, generally between April and June for fall applicants.

What BU Looks For

  • At least 12 completed college credits (12+ credit applicants apply as transfers; fewer, and the first-year path applies instead)
  • A strong, consistent college GPA — the single most heavily weighted factor, with admitted students often at 3.5 or higher
  • A declared major within one of BU's degree-granting schools or colleges — transfer applicants cannot apply undeclared
  • Fit with the specific program applied to — Questrom School of Business, the College of Engineering, and Sargent College tend to be the most competitive for transfers

Requirements & Deadlines

  • Common Application (Transfer version), due February 1 for fall entry or October 1 for spring entry
  • Official transcripts from every college attended, plus a high school transcript if fewer than 30 college credits are completed
  • One letter of recommendation from a college instructor familiar with the applicant's work
  • No SAT/ACT scores required for transfer admission
  • Decisions released on a rolling basis, generally April through June for fall applicants

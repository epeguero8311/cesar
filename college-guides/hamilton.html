Overview

Hamilton College's first-year admissions process is evaluated separately from its transfer process, with its own applicant pool, review committee, and timeline. Most highly selective schools admit first-year applicants at a meaningfully different rate than transfers, so the numbers above should not be assumed to mirror the transfer figures below.

Applicants generally apply through Early Decision, Early Action, or Regular Decision depending on the school's specific offerings, and should weigh the tradeoffs of each round carefully, since binding Early Decision plans and non-binding Early Action or Restrictive Early Action plans have different implications for financial aid and other applications.

What Hamilton Looks For

  • A rigorous high school course load, including the most advanced classes available (AP, IB, honors, or dual enrollment)
  • Strong, consistent grades throughout high school, particularly in junior and senior year
  • Meaningful extracurricular involvement, leadership, or personal responsibilities that show depth rather than a long list of shallow activities
  • Thoughtful, authentic essays that reveal genuine voice and self-reflection
  • Strong letters of recommendation from teachers and counselors who know the applicant well

Requirements & Deadlines

  • Common Application, Coalition Application, or the school's own first-year application, submitted by the relevant deadline above
  • Official high school transcript and school profile
  • Letters of recommendation from a counselor and one or more teachers
  • Standardized testing policy varies by school and by year — confirm current SAT/ACT requirements before applying
  • Decisions released according to the applicant's chosen round (Early Decision/Action vs. Regular Decision)

Overview

Hamilton's transfer acceptance rate has trended upward somewhat in recent cycles, landing around 12% for the most recent confirmed year after running closer to 5-8% just a couple years earlier.

Hamilton is one of the relatively few highly selective liberal arts colleges to offer both fall and spring transfer entry, enrolling about 10-20 students in the fall and 5-10 in the spring each year.

What Hamilton Looks For

  • Strong recent college coursework, since Hamilton weighs the most recent academic work most heavily
  • Standardized SAT or ACT scores, which are required for all transfer applicants
  • A College Instructor Evaluation and a College Official's Report
  • A personal essay addressing reasons for transferring and fit with Hamilton's open curriculum
  • Optional but strongly encouraged interviews, typically offered virtually

Requirements & Deadlines

  • Common Application or Coalition Application, due March 16 for fall entry or November 1 for spring entry
  • Official transcripts from every college attended, plus a final high school transcript
  • College Instructor Evaluation, College Official's Report, and standardized test scores
  • A personal essay
  • Decisions released on a rolling basis as applications are completed
